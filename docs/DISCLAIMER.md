# Safety & Legal Disclaimer

**Read this before deploying or selling CarTalk.**

CarTalk drives a message display mounted in a vehicle. Using it involves real
safety and legal considerations. The software is provided as-is, with no
warranty (see `LICENSE`). You — the deployer, seller, or driver — are solely
responsible for safe and lawful use.

## Driving safety

- **Do not compose while driving.** CarTalk includes safety features
  (speed-aware locking, one-tap presets, hands-free voice, auto-clearing
  messages) to reduce distraction, but no software can make in-vehicle
  interaction risk-free. Compose while stopped, or let a passenger operate it.
- The **speed-aware lock**, **hard-brake auto-reaction**, and speed readings
  rely on phone sensors (GPS/accelerometer) that can be inaccurate, delayed, or
  unavailable. Never rely on them as safety-critical systems.
- Messages and animations on a rear display can distract **other** drivers. Keep
  content brief, clear, and non-provocative.

## The "Emergency" messages are just text

The `CALL 911 / HELP / MEDICAL / BABY IN CAR / HAZARD` signs display text on the
screen only. **They do not contact emergency services, police, or anyone.** In a
real emergency, call your local emergency number directly.

## Legal / regulatory

- **Many jurisdictions regulate or prohibit** electronic/scrolling/illuminated
  signs and displays on vehicles, especially rear-facing or animated ones, and
  some restrict anything resembling official signals (brake, turn, emergency).
  Laws vary by country, state, and municipality and change over time.
- It is the deployer's responsibility to confirm that a given display, message
  set, mounting, and brightness are legal where the vehicle is operated.
- If you sell CarTalk, make buyers aware of the above and do not represent it as
  road-legal anywhere.

## Data & privacy

- **Live-map sharing is opt-in and off by default.** When enabled, the app sends
  the on-screen message and the phone's location to the relay you configure. The
  relay coarsens coordinates (~11 m) and stores no identity or history, but you
  are responsible for your privacy policy, consent, and applicable data laws
  (e.g. GDPR/CCPA) if you operate a shared relay for others.
- Voice input is transcribed by the device/browser speech service; review those
  providers' terms.
